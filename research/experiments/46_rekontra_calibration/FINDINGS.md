# exp46 — the soloist's rekontra is NOT a leak

**Verdict: negative result. The deployed rekontra rule is within 0.14 GP/unit of the best
threshold policy available from its own signal. Do not change it.**

This started from a real game (`2d26851c23a2`, 2026-08-02): the AI bid a piros ulti that
god confirms *was* makeable, threw it away on the opening card, and then rekontra'd with
`op_unit_makeability_post1` reporting **1.000** on a contract already double-dummy dead.
−96 GP. The hypothesis was that the estimator suffers the same disease exp27 fixed for
defenders — a systematically wrong makeability driving a bad doubling rule.

It does not. Measured over **5,486 unit-positions** from the exp43 corpus, re-running the
production estimator at each post-trick-1 position and comparing to the realised outcome.

## The estimator is calibrated on average, optimistic where it decides

overall: mean estimate 0.695 · actual make 0.645 · **optimism +0.050**

| estimate bin | n | mean est | actual | gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [0.00,0.20) | 1036 | 0.047 | 0.185 | −0.138 |
| [0.20,0.40) | 362 | 0.331 | 0.370 | −0.040 |
| [0.40,0.60) | 394 | 0.499 | 0.470 | +0.029 |
| [0.60,0.80) | 439 | 0.668 | 0.597 | +0.071 |
| [0.80,0.95) | 596 | 0.833 | 0.705 | +0.128 |
| **[0.95,1.00)** | **2659** | **1.000** | **0.882** | **+0.118** |

Same shape exp41 found in the bidding heads: fine on average, wrong at the top, and the
top is where the decision lives. Half of all positions land in the ≥0.95 bin, and 11.8% of
those fail. **milan's game was one of those 314 — an unlucky draw from a bet that is
correct 88.2% of the time, not a bug.**

Per unit, the optimism concentrates where it costs least: betli +0.252, durchmars +0.094,
20-100 +0.069 — against ulti +0.045, 40-100 −0.015, parti −0.030.

## …but it does not matter, because the payoff is forgiving

Conditional on a unit being kontra'd, the soloist takes `iso[1]` by declining and `iso[2]`
by rekontra-ing, so the optimal policy is computable exactly from the recorded payoffs —
no model, no calibration assumption:

| policy | fires | soloist GP/unit |
|---|---|---|
| never rekontra | 0% | +1.165 |
| always rekontra | 100% | +2.678 |
| **DEPLOYED** (`sol_ev(est,0) > 0`) | 65% | **+6.542** |
| theory (`est > 0.5`) | 67% | +6.631 |
| swept optimum (`est > 0.76`) | 59% | +6.686 |

**Perfect threshold tuning is worth +0.144 GP per kontra'd unit.** The rule survives the
estimator's optimism because rekontra-ing at a true 88.2% is simply correct: the level-1
vs level-2 comparison breaks even at P(make) = 0.5 for every contract, including bid ulti
whose asymmetric bukott cancels. An estimate that says 1.00 when the truth is 0.90 is on
the right side of 0.5 either way.

The deployed rule asks "is my no-kontra EV positive", which for a bid ulti means p > 0.667
rather than the correct p > 0.5 — conservative by construction. That conservatism happens
to offset the optimism almost exactly. Two errors, opposite signs, near-cancelling.

| 20-100 | 284 | +12.45 | +12.56 | est>0.34 | +0.11 |

The two real gains — betli +2.52 and durchmars +0.93 — sit on units the **deployed
defenders never kontra at all** (exp27: betli, 20-100 and trumpless duri abstain). Those
rows are counterfactual: the sweep asks "if this were kontra'd, what should the soloist
do", and for betli and colorless duri the antecedent is false in play. Realisable gain is
therefore ulti +0.42 and 40-100 +0.50, on the minority of deals where those units get
kontra'd at all — a fraction of a GP per deal.

## What this means

1. **Close the rekontra thread.** It was the cheapest-looking item on the kontra track and
   it is worth ~0.14 GP/unit. The GP in milan's −96 game came from the *opening card*, not
   the doubling.
2. **The failure mode is real but is a play problem.** Per-world double-dummy does let the
   soloist tailor a line it cannot tailor in practice, which is why the top bin reads 1.000
   instead of 0.882. Fixing that means a realistic-play estimator, and exp40 already showed
   what happens when realistic labels meet a near-perfect opponent. Not worth it for 0.14 GP.
3. **Caveat on the corpus.** exp43's deals were generated by the pre-fix (talon-peeking,
   uncalibrated) bidder, which biases *which contracts appear*. It does not bias the
   relationship between an estimate and the outcome at a given position, which is all this
   measures — but the per-unit row counts are not the deployed frequency mix.
